# MSEDCLREtransitionOptimizer
This calculator optimizes the renewable energy plant capacity for given consumption pattern and generation per MW pattern as per ToD slot of MSEDCL.
This tool tells you, in plain rupee terms, whether buying solar or wind power through "open access" (generating your own green power and wheeling it to your premises) is worth it for your business — and for how long.
Start on the left, under Your project. Pick your electricity tariff category (the same one printed on your MSEDCL bill), your connection voltage, and whether the plant is solar or wind. Then enter two things: how much power the plant generates each month per MW, split across the three daily time-slots — night/morning, daytime "solar hours", and evening "peak" — and how many units your business consumes each month in those same slots. Finally add the money side: the plant's cost per MW, how much of it is equity, your share of that equity, and the return you expect on your money.
Now click the green Optimise plant capacity button. It automatically finds the plant size that saves you the most while staying within the rules, and fills in the capacity for you.
Read the right side top to bottom. The first big number is your net saving per month. The second big number is the discounted payback — the years of savings needed to earn back the money you invested. Below it, a coloured recommendation tells you what to do: green means a long-term contract is safe, amber means sign only a shorter medium-term contract, red means don't go ahead.
Change the Financial year dropdown to see how savings shift each year to 2029-30 — they often shrink over time. For the full detail — slot-by-slot savings, the year-by-year table, and the cost build-up — click Show the full working. Everything recalculates instantly as you edit, so try different assumptions freely and watch the numbers respond.
